# Evidence

Keep a short digest for the current task or recent work. Group by claim,
section, or behavior.

- Research: quotation or paraphrase, source and locator, what it
  supports, and any material qualification. Label your own inference.
- Code: behavior or revision checked, command or run link, observed
  result, and relevant limits.

Replace superseded entries. Keep full source notes, raw logs, and any
needed history outside this digest.
