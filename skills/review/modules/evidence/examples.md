# Evidence examples

## Source fit (illustrative)

A draft claims a training program improves retention. Its cited randomized
study measures immediate test scores, not retention. The design can support an
immediate performance effect, but the outcome and follow-up do not establish
retention. Seek a source measuring retention or narrow the claim. Conversely,
a study measuring delayed recall could support the claim even if it uses the
term “delayed recall” rather than “retention,” depending on the intended meaning.
