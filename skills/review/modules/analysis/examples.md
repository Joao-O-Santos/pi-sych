# Analysis examples

Input: an article makes causal language from a cross-sectional association.

Output: identify the design-to-claim gap and offer calibrated interpretation rather than a wholesale rewrite.

## Reporting-language check

Input:

> The treatment group had a significant improvement over the control group.

Output:

> Check whether the claim should compare different groups ("people assigned to
> treatment performed better than people assigned to control") or the same
> people across conditions. Separate the observed difference from the evidence
> supplied by the statistical test, and scope the inferential claim to the
> population or process justified by the design and sampling.
