# Copyedit

Use after structure and argument are stable. Check grammar, agreement,
punctuation, terminology, labels, ambiguous pronouns, modifiers,
nominalizations, filler, parallelism, and figure or table callouts. Apply the
routed prose method for information flow, rhythm, and voice.

Default to minimum intervention. Preserve a clear, accurate, audience-appropriate
sentence that fits the controlling style, even when another wording would sound
more polished to the model. Do not homogenize sentence length, erase useful
idiosyncrasy, or replace authored prose with generic professional prose.
Diagnose the construction before commenting on passive voice and identify
whether agency matters.

For each material finding state location, concern, why it matters, and the
minimum local wording or consistency response. Report concrete local drift from
the controlling style rather than applying generic rules. Preserve intentional
fragments, dialect, emphasis, hedging, and variation unless they obstruct the
artifact's purpose.
