# Sources examples

## Discovery is not inspection

Input: a local index and an OpenAlex-like service both identify the same paper as
relevant.

Output: use that agreement to prioritize the paper, not to claim that the paper
supports the target proposition. Inspect the paper itself with an available
full-text/PDF reader before reporting its exact method, result, limitation, or
quotation.

## Known correction

Input: a blog and a corrected primary paper disagree.

Output: inspect the corrected primary evidence and, where useful, the publisher
or correction page directly. Explain the conflict and retain the blog only as
context if relevant.

## Partial access

Input: metadata and an abstract are available, but the full paper cannot be
retrieved.

Output: report what metadata and abstract-level claims can actually establish,
state the access limitation, and do not reconstruct full methods or results from
background knowledge or secondary summaries.
