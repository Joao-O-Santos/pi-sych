# Citations

Check claim-to-source alignment, provenance, quotation accuracy, and retrieval limits. Cite what the source actually supports and identify claims that still need a source. Verify author, title, date, version, locator, and retraction or correction status where material. Quote only text inspected in the cited version; paraphrase scope and qualification rather than converting a tentative source into a stronger claim. Keep source statements separate from synthesis and identify citations that are illustrative rather than evidential.
