# European Portuguese

Target pt-PT, not a generic Portuguese default. Classify a suspected issue as an error, Brazilian usage, register difference, accepted variant, or preference before changing it. Check clitic placement, `estar a + infinitive`, vocabulary, forms of address, agreement, prepositions, collocations, punctuation, and terminology.

Common pt-BR intrusions include gerund-heavy progressive forms where `estar a + infinitive` fits, `você` in contexts requiring another address form, and vocabulary such as `arquivo` where `ficheiro` is intended. Do not mechanically ban forms that are valid in Portugal.
