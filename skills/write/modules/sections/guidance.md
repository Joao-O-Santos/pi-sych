# Sections

Before drafting, decide the reader, the section's job, its central claim or
decision, available evidence, and remaining uncertainty. Calibrate voice,
format, citation practice, and explanation to audience and genre. Give every
section a job, make transitions between jobs visible, and preserve citations,
placeholders, uncertainty, and authorial variation.

For an abstract state the problem, approach, contribution or result, and
implication without adding content. An introduction establishes the problem and
intellectual move. Methods make consequential design and analysis decisions
inspectable. Results report the supported pattern without importing discussion.
Discussion synthesizes rather than repeats, considers serious alternatives,
states concrete limits and next tests, and ends with a calibrated take-home. A
conclusion should resolve the artifact's job rather than add a generic
importance claim.

Use titles that foreground a surprising or consequential move without
clickbait or false promise. Use headings and transitions to expose the sequence,
not to narrate the document mechanically. Follow the project's Markdown and
renderer configuration when it applies. Run a formatter and inspect its diff
before reporting formatter work; do not add a dependency solely for prose
formatting.
