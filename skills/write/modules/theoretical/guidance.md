# Theoretical

Organize by the argument's logic, not IMRaD. Usually open with a concrete
puzzle; state the contribution or mechanism; define needed constructs; then show
how inputs produce outcomes and which assumptions carry the account. Adjust the
order when the genre or argument requires it.

Use the routed argument method when it helps contrast serious alternatives,
specify scope and boundary failures, or expose predictions that discriminate
rivals. Use examples to illuminate mechanisms, never as empirical evidence. End
with what the account reveals and cannot capture. Label premises, illustrations,
inferences, and unresolved choices where that distinction matters; polished
prose must not turn a conceptual claim into a finding.
