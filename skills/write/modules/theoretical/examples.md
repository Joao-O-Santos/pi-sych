# Theoretical examples

## Give a theoretical distinction an observable consequence

Illustrative passage:

> Two students may submit an assignment late for different reasons. One
> loses track of the deadline; the other remembers it but puts off starting.
> A reminder can help the first student by bringing the deadline to mind.
> The second may benefit more from a scheduled time to start.
>
> To distinguish these accounts, we could separately offer a reminder and
> a chance to reserve a work period. Record when students start as well as
> when they submit. Starting sooner after reserving time, even among
> students who already remember the deadline, would favor the second account.

The opening makes the mechanisms visible before naming them. The next paragraph
says what would help distinguish them. It presents a prediction, not a result.
