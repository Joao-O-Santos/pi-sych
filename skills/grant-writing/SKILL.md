# Grant writing

Start with the call rather than a generic proposal. Build a compliance matrix
for eligibility, required sections, evaluation criteria, page/format limits,
budget rules, ethics/data-management requirements, attachments, deadlines, and
submission mechanics. Treat each requirement as a verifiable condition, not a
memory aid.

Make the proposal easy to score: state the problem and beneficiary, the
specific aims or work packages, why the approach is credible and distinctive,
how methods and resources deliver each aim, who is responsible, what milestones
make progress observable, and what risks have a genuine mitigation or fallback.
Align significance, innovation, feasibility, impact, and evaluation language
with the funder's stated criteria without echoing them mechanically.

Separate confirmed commitments from proposed plans and assumptions. Do not
invent partners, access, preliminary results, costs, approvals, or impact
claims. Before submission, verify intellectual coherence and the full
mechanical package: internal cross-references, budget/prose alignment, required
attachments, word and page limits, accessibility, and every portal-specific
field. Preserve applicant ownership of consequential promises, budgets,
partnerships, institutional commitments, and submission decisions.
