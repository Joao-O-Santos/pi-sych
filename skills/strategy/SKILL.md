# Strategy

Choose an integrated response, an implementation order, and global constraints.
Explicitly identify findings not to apply and concepts, abstractions, or
qualifications not to add. Prefer a simpler coherent revision over a set of
locally optimal changes that cause sprawl.

## Optional decision memo

When several options compete or a human decision is required, use a short memo:

1. **Options** — the viable responses under consideration.
2. **Evidence** — observed findings, constraints, and checks that bear on them.
3. **Trade-offs** — what each option gains, loses, or risks.
4. **Unresolved questions** — what still needs owner input.
5. **Owner decision** — leave blank or provisional until the human decides.

Do not treat the memo as approval. Consequential choices still need ordinary
conversation or `submit_plan` when formal review helps.

## Use with

Use after `artifact-review` or optional review lenses when findings interact.
Use `reconcile-project` when the decision changes tracked project state, and
`verify-change` after the chosen response is implemented.
