# Structural review

Use this lens with `artifact-review` when the question is whether an artifact
helps a reader see its purpose, sequence, and intellectual move. Do not
redesign the artifact or treat a preferred structure as universally required.

## Check

- Does the opening establish the problem, puzzle, decision, or consequence?
- Is the central contribution visible early enough and repeated where a reader
  needs orientation?
- Does each section have a job, a useful order, and proportionate length?
- Do topic sentences, transitions, headings, and signposts make the reasoning
  legible?
- Are competing perspectives treated fairly rather than reduced to a straw
  position?

## Findings

For each material finding, state the location, concern, why it matters, and
minimum response. Distinguish a defect from a genre choice or preference.

## Use with

Load with `artifact-review` as the core. Pair with `review-detail` for claim
and evidence scrutiny, `review-copyedit` after structure is sound, and
`strategy` when several findings interact.
