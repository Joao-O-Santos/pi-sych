# Quantitative examples

Input: a regression table reports a p-value without effect size or interval.

Output: report the estimand, estimate, uncertainty, assumptions checked, and what the design does not establish.

## Reporting scope

Bad:

> The sample showed a significant difference between A and B.

Better, repeated measures:

> The results provide evidence that people in the target population perform
> better in A than in B.

Better, independent groups:

> The results provide evidence that people in A perform better than people in B
> in the target population represented by the study.

Why: the observed sample difference is descriptive regardless of the threshold.
The statistical result bears on an inferential claim, and the wording must
preserve whether the same or different units contributed to A and B.
