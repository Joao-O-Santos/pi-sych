# Detail review

Use this lens with `artifact-review` when the question is whether claims,
concepts, and inferences hold under skeptical inspection. Raise alternatives as
questions, not accusations. Do not redesign the artifact.

## Check

- Are central claims supported by identified evidence, methods, analyses, or
  explicitly labelled premises?
- Are key terms used consistently and defined only as far as the argument needs?
- Have contrary, reverse-causal, common-cause, selection, measurement, or
  moderator accounts been considered where they matter?
- Name a fallacy or statistical concern specifically when present; explain why
  it weakens the inference without treating it as proof the conclusion is false.
- Frame bias checks as "check whether" questions about HARKing, selective
  reporting, omitted nulls, post-hoc exclusions, or uncorrected multiplicity.
- For quantitative work, watch for correlation presented as causation, base-rate
  neglect, non-significance treated as no effect, significance treated as
  importance, and missing uncertainty or sample justification.

## Findings

For each material finding, state the location, concern, why it matters, and
minimum response. Distinguish a missing citation, a weak inference, an
unresolved alternative, and an authorial choice.

## Use with

Load with `artifact-review` as the core. Pair with `review-structure` when
organization is still open, `theory-development` when alternatives need
generative expansion, and `strategy` when findings interact.
