# Artifact workflow

Use one general loop: brief → explore or structure → build → targeted review when justified → integrated strategy → revise → verify → meaningful brief update. Adapt the depth to risk; do not force every phase onto simple reversible work. Artifact skills change guidance and verification, not the workflow engine.

Reviewers diagnose, strategists choose one coherent response, and verifiers judge the resulting artifact globally. Preserve human ownership of architecture, public APIs, central claims, publication, deployment, irreversible changes, security-sensitive behavior, and new dependencies unless explicitly delegated.
