# Claim-and-evidence examples

## Compound claim

Sentence:

> The intervention improved recall and eliminates long-term relapse (Lee,
> 2024).

Inspected source:

> Reports improved immediate recall; does not measure relapse.

Response:

Retain and calibrate the recall clause. Remove, qualify, or find support for the
relapse clause. Correct citation format does not support the unsupported claim.

## Numerical trace

Bad:

> Accuracy increased by 12%.

Better claim map:

- claim: accuracy increased by 12 percentage points;
- support: `outputs/model-3.csv`, contrast row 4;
- scope: analyzed sample after stated exclusions;
- limitation: interval includes effects smaller than the prose implies.

Why:

The map distinguishes percent from percentage points and connects wording to an
inspectable output.

## Citation roles

A historical example may illustrate a mechanism without testing it. Label that
citation illustrative. A methods paper may justify a procedure without
supporting the study's empirical conclusion. Label its role rather than
counting both as generic support.
