# Prose examples

## Reverse outline before line editing

Draft paragraphs currently do these jobs:

1. define the construct;
2. repeat the definition with a new citation;
3. introduce the mechanism;
4. return to a boundary condition needed by paragraph 1.

A useful response is to merge 1 and 2, move the boundary condition beside the
definition, and retain 3 as the next move. Line editing first would hide the
structural duplication.

## Ordinary paragraph and exception

Bad:

> At the present juncture, it is important to note that the archive is not
> merely incomplete—it is also, in many respects, fragmented. This significant
> limitation underscores the importance of exercising caution.

Better:

> The archive is incomplete and uneven. Missing correspondence limits what we
> can say about why the policy changed, although the surviving minutes still
> show when the change occurred.

Why:

The revision gives the paragraph one evidential job in a natural register. It
removes ceremonial framing, canned contrast, and a generic conclusion without
hiding the limitation.

## When the correct edit is no edit

Source:

> Most participants noticed the change. A few did not, and that difference
> matters for what follows.

Unhelpful rewrite:

> Although the majority of participants detected the change, a minority did
> not, highlighting an important distinction for the subsequent analysis.

Better response:

> Leave the source unchanged.

Why:

The source is already clear, accurate, economical, and rhythmically varied.
The rewrite adds formality and abstraction without solving a problem.

## Drafting toward an established voice

Suppose several author examples use direct topic sentences, mostly ordinary
vocabulary, occasional short sentences for emphasis, and longer sentences only
when several ideas genuinely need to be related. A new paragraph should follow
that distribution. It should not copy memorable phrases from the examples, and
it should not smooth every sentence toward the same medium-length cadence.

## Voice

Useful passive:

> Responses were recorded at 10 ms intervals.

Agency matters:

> The committee removed the excluded cases after inspecting the outcome.

Not a passive:

> The estimate is uncertain.

The first keeps the measurement procedure as topic, the second names a
consequential actor, and the third uses a copular construction rather than
passive voice.
